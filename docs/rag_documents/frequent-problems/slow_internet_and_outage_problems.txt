MNNIT Network Support Knowledge Base
Category: Frequently encountered network problems
Topic: Slow internet and internet down in a whole room or hostel
Note: The student steps below can be sent to the student by SMS or WhatsApp.


PROBLEM: The internet is very slow

Steps for the student:
- Stop big downloads running in the background.
- Run only one speed test at a time.
- Check the condition of the Wi-Fi signal and the LAN connection.
- Check the internet speed on another device at the same time.
- Send the time of the problem, your Hostel/Room Number and a screenshot of the speed test.
If the internet is slow in many rooms, please give us that information.

Technician: Check AP utilization, switch uplink, bandwidth usage, packet loss and Proxy load.


PROBLEM: The internet is down in the whole room or the whole Hostel

Please check first:
- Are both Wi-Fi and LAN down in your room?
- Is the internet also down in the other rooms nearby?
- If possible, check from another device.
- Send the Hostel Name, Room Number, the time the problem started and the status of Wi-Fi/LAN.

If the internet is down in many rooms at the same time, instead of changing various settings, report it to the Technician.

Technician: Check the AP/uplink, distribution switch, OFC link, DHCP, core routing and the related Proxy/Internet gateway.
