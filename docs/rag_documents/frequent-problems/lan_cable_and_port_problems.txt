Topic: LAN cable connected but Ethernet not working
Note: The student steps below can be sent to the student by SMS or WhatsApp.


PROBLEM: The LAN cable is plugged in, but Ethernet is not working

Steps for the student:
- Plug the LAN cable properly into the laptop and the wall socket.
- Look at the cable/network status in the Ethernet icon.
- If possible, test with another good LAN cable.
- Run ipconfig in CMD and look at the IPv4 Address of the Ethernet adapter.
- If it shows "Network cable unplugged" or no IP is obtained, send a screenshot.
Please do not open the wiring of the wall socket or the switch yourself.

Technician's check: Check the switch port link, VLAN assignment, port security, DHCP and the cable/socket.
