MNNIT Network Support Knowledge Base
Category: Device manuals and standard operating procedures
Topic: Easy decision method for the Technician when a student complaint is received

1. The student's complaint is received.
2. Ask: Is the problem also on other devices/rooms?
3. If only one device is affected: check the IP, Wi-Fi, Proxy, Browser and Adapter.
4. If many devices/rooms are affected: check the AP, Switch, OFC, DHCP, Proxy and upstream.
5. Confirm that the problem is resolved and close the complaint.
